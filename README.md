# 🧠 Mindify

**Your AI-powered mental wellness companion — built by Team DataGrains**

Mindify is an empathetic AI chatbot that listens to your stress, understands your emotions, and offers real, CBT-based support — through text or voice, anytime, anywhere.

---

## 🚩 The Problem

- 75% of people experience daily stress but lack access to real emotional support.
- Therapy is often expensive or intimidating.
- Most existing apps feel robotic — not truly empathetic.
- There's a need for an AI that understands emotions like a human does.

---

## 💡 The Idea / Solution

- Built with **Gemini 2.0 Flash + Qdrant**
- Understands both **text and voice** input
- Detects emotions, offers **CBT-based advice**, and recalls personalized insights
- Feels like talking to a caring therapist — anytime, anywhere

---

## ⚙️ How It Works (Flow)

1. User speaks or types about their stress
2. Gemini analyzes the emotion & context
3. Qdrant retrieves related psychology / CBT book insights
4. Gemini crafts an empathetic, grounded response

---

## 🎨 User Experience

- **Text input:** Users can type their thoughts directly into the chat box ("Share your thoughts...") and get an empathetic, CBT-grounded response.
- **Voice input:** Users can drag & drop or browse to upload audio files (WAV / MP3 / M4A, up to 200MB per file) and click **Submit Audio** — Mindify transcribes and responds just like it would to text.
- **Live feedback:** A "Mindify is thinking..." indicator shows while a response is being generated.
- Responses include practical stress-coping tips, next steps, and key insights drawn from psychology/CBT texts (e.g., *Calm Your Thoughts* by Nick Trenton).
